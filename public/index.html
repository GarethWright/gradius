Flight manual

Clear seven sectors and destroy each guardian. Your ship has three lives. Every 20,000 points earns another.

Destroy orange enemies and complete red formations to collect power capsules. Each capsule advances the meter. Press X or tap Upgrade to equip the highlighted item.

  • Speed: Faster movement (up to 3).
  • Missile: Ground-skimming missiles.
  • Double / Laser: Angled shots or piercing beams. Equipping one replaces the other.
  • Option: A following drone that mirrors your fire (up to 4).
  • Shield: Absorbs three hits.

On touch screens, use the direction pad and hold Fire, or drag your ship directly to move and fire.

A familiar secret…

Enter ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A on the title screen or while paused. On mobile use the direction pad and B / A buttons. Grants full equipment and 30 ships.
